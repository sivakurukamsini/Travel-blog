Frequently Asked Questions

Question Answer
What is the best time to visit Sri Lanka? The best time is from December to March for the west and south coasts, and May to September for the east coast.
Do I need a visa to enter Sri Lanka? Most tourists can obtain an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) online before arrival. Some nationalities can get visa on arrival.
What is the currency of Sri Lanka? The currency is the Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). You can exchange money at the airport or use ATMs.
Is Sri Lanka a safe destination? Yes, Sri Lanka is generally considered safe for tourists. Exercise normal precautions like in any other country.
What language is spoken in Sri Lanka? Sinhala is the official language. English is widely spoken, especially in tourist areas.
How long should I stay in Sri Lanka? 7-10 days is ideal for covering major destinations. You can do a 3-4 day trip or extend if you want to relax more.
